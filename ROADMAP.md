# Roadmap

> Generated from `config/roadmap.json` by `node scripts/roadmap.mjs`. Live version: https://tlp-icons.netlify.app/roadmap/

**Goal: 26,180 animated icons by 2028.** Today: 6,620 (25%).

## Principles

- Every new icon is animated, matches the 24x24 / 2px / round-cap style and passes the test suite.
- Field depth first: a developer building a legal, medical or finance product should find every icon they need in one place.
- Hand-drawn tlp originals for missing concepts; new open-source sets only after license review and owner approval.
- Each release ships a frame-strip review sheet so motion quality is checked by eye, not only by tests.

## Phases

### Foundation (Q4 2026, 8,000 icons)

- Legal field to 150 originals (courts, contracts, police, documents)
- Health to 300: clinical, dental, lab, pharmacy
- Finance to 300: banking, payments, regional currencies
- Brands kept in sync with Tabler

### Professional fields (Q1-Q2 2027, 14,000 icons)

- Legal to 600: case management, procedure, legal entities
- Education & science to 600
- Commerce & logistics to 700
- Government and public services
- Filled style as a second variant (owner decision)

### Depth everywhere (Q3 2027 - Q2 2028, 26,180 icons)

- Every category at its target (legal 1,180)
- Regional sets: Arabic letters and numerals, regional currencies and IDs
- Icon request board: community votes decide the next batch
- Figma plugin and per-framework wrappers
